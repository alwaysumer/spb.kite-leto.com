About kitesurfing

Kitesurfing in St Petersburg: where and when

Kitesurfing (also called kiteboarding or kiting) means riding a board pulled by a kite. You do not need to travel to the sea to learn: the Gulf of Finland is right next to the city, so you can learn to kite in St Petersburg itself.

Our kite school works on Sestroretsk beach in the Kurortny district. It has a wide sandy shore and shallow water, so beginners can take their first steps comfortably and safely: launching the kite on the beach and learning to control it in the water.

The season on the water runs from late spring to autumn. We ride on windy days and choose the time by the wind forecast so that every lesson is worthwhile.

We teach complete beginners to the IKO programme, help those who started at another school to finish their training, and rent kites and boards to confident riders. In winter the school moves to Vietnam, where there is wind all year round.

FAQ

Kitesurfing in St Petersburg: FAQ

How much does it cost to learn kiteboarding in St Petersburg?+

An intro lesson (1 hour) is 6,000 ₽, a 2-hour lesson 10,000 ₽ and the basic course (6 hours) 30,000 ₽. Equipment is included.

Where do the lessons take place?+

On Sestroretsk beach (Kurortnaya street, Sestroretsk), on the Gulf of Finland. We ride on windy days.

What equipment do I need to start?+

Nothing. The school provides all the equipment and it is included in the price.

Can I learn without being physically fit?+

Yes. You hold the kite with your body weight and harness, not arm strength. Kids and women learn easily and then ride on their own.

Can I study the theory remotely?+

Yes, you can read materials or watch video lessons in advance — it speeds up learning a lot.

Can I continue after another school?+

Yes. You can finish your training with us on good terms without repeating the hours you have already done. Message us on Telegram for details.

How many lessons do I need to learn?+

Usually about 10 hours with an instructor to start riding on your own. The basic course (6 hours) covers all the basics and your first metres on the board; then you can add 1–2 hour lessons.

When is the kitesurfing season in St Petersburg?+

On the water, the season runs from late spring to autumn. We ride on windy days and agree on the time in advance based on the wind forecast.

How do I book a lesson?+

Message us on Telegram @kifesurf or email kiteleto2010@gmail.com and we will pick a windy day and a course for your level.

Can I learn on my own?+

It is possible — there is plenty of material online. But counting the time, the risks to you and others and the cost of gear, self-teaching makes little sense.
